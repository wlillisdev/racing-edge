# THE COMMENTS TEST — pre-registered, 2026-09-20, before any comment was read

The master has said since August that MANNER OF RUNNING is the lens that
matters: "the one winning consistently", the difference between a horse that
kept on and one that found nothing. The counting study of 7 September named
it as the one lens the corpus could not see. Today four other ideas were
tested properly and all four failed; this is the last candidate with a real
argument behind it, and the last one I will propose.

**This page is written before a single comment has been read.** The word
lists, the rules and the bar are fixed here so that neither of us can move
them once the numbers appear. A rule chosen after seeing the data is not a
finding, it is a story — that is how the +21% "beaten far last time" lead
was born this morning and how it died by lunchtime (−19.2% on unseen races).

## WHY THIS LENS AND NOT ANOTHER

The beaten-lengths proxy tested earlier today failed. It had to: a bare
number cannot say **why** a horse finished where it did. A horse beaten five
lengths after being badly hampered ran better than one beaten two while
emptying out. The figure says the opposite of the truth; the comment knows.

Comments are text, not figures. They are awkward to price mechanically, they
sit behind the results door, and they are the one place a human reader might
plausibly see something the market has not already arbitraged away.

## THE THREE SIGNALS (fixed now)

Read from a horse's PREVIOUS runs only. This race's comment is the answer
sheet and is never shown to the scorer.

**TROUBLE** — the finishing position understates the run:
`hampered`, `no clear run`, `not clear run`, `short of room`, `checked`,
`squeezed`, `denied`, `badly outpaced early`, `slowly away`, `stumbled`

**GALLANT** — finished better than it started:
`stayed on`, `kept on`, `ran on`, `rallied`, `finished well`, `just held`,
`every chance`, `challenged`, `led close home`

**EMPTY** — stopped:
`weakened`, `found little`, `no extra`, `one paced`, `faded`, `tailed off`,
`outpaced`, `hung`, `never a factor`, `always behind`, `no impression`

A comment may carry more than one signal; each is counted independently.
Matching is case-insensitive substring on the comment text.

## THE RULES TO BE TESTED — SIX, AND NO MORE

Each is scored on the favourite unless stated, because the favourite is the
only horse whose fair price we can state confidently.

| # | rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | favourite whose LAST comment carries TROUBLE |
| 2 | favourite whose LAST comment carries GALLANT |
| 3 | favourite whose LAST comment carries EMPTY — expected to be a NEGATIVE screen |
| 4 | favourite with TROUBLE or GALLANT and NOT EMPTY |
| 5 | favourite with EMPTY in **two** of its last three runs — the avoid |
| 6 | shortest-priced horse in the race carrying TROUBLE last time (not necessarily the favourite) |

Six rules. The report must say six.

## THE BAR — all four, or it is not a finding

1. **n ≥ 100** in the held-out half.
2. **Beats the SP-favourite benchmark on the same races**, on strike AND on
   return (his correction of 7 September).
3. **Positive on the held-out period**, which is everything after
   2026-08-14 — races that did not exist when this project began looking.
4. **The direction is the one predicted here**, not its opposite discovered
   afterwards.

## THE STOPPING RULE

If nothing clears the bar, that is the end of the search. I will not propose
a seventh rule, a different word list, or another lens. The honest conclusion
would be that the data this project owns contains no detectable edge, and the
recommendation would be to stop.

And if something DOES clear it: treat it with suspicion, not enthusiasm. It
is the fifth idea tested today. Five tests at the usual thresholds carry
roughly a one-in-five chance of a false positive, and this morning's lead
looked far better than anything that is likely to come out of this.

# THE RESULT — 2026-09-20, after the test was run

**Six rules tried, exactly the six pre-registered above. Nothing survives.**

Corpus: 6,184 races, 32,560 run comments. Tune = on or before 2026-08-14.
Held out = after it, races that did not exist when this hunt began.

| rule | tune n | tune ROI | held-out n | held-out ROI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 fav, last comment TROUBLE | 91 | −9.9% | 104 | **+23.7%** |
| 2 fav, last comment GALLANT | 414 | **+13.4%** | 450 | **−10.0%** |
| 3 fav, last comment EMPTY | 73 | −3.5% | 74 | +6.6% |
| 4 fav, TROUBLE/GALLANT not EMPTY | 457 | **+10.3%** | 493 | −4.6% |
| 5 fav, EMPTY in 2 of last 3 | 3 | −100% | 8 | −20.2% |
| 6 shortest w/ TROUBLE last time | 445 | −36.0% | 504 | −20.9% |

Benchmark, all favourites: tune −4.0% (n=4829), held out −5.1% (n=1355).

## WHAT RULE 2 PROVES, AND IT IS THE POINT OF THE WHOLE DAY

"Back the favourite whose last run says it stayed on" is the master's own
lens, almost word for word. It returned **+13.4% over 414 bets** on the half
the hunt was allowed to see, and **−10.0% over 450 bets** on the half it was
not. A twenty-three point reversal on a large sample.

Had only the first half existed — which is precisely what months of reading
races gives a person — this would have been carved as a rule, with a
paragraph of reasoning and a test pinning it shut, and it would have lost
money quietly all autumn while the losses were explained away.

## RULE 1 IS REFUSED, AND THE BAR ABOVE IS ADMITTED TO BE TOO WEAK

Rule 1 clears the four conditions written at the top of this page: n≥100 held
out, beats the benchmark on strike and return, positive held out, direction
as predicted. **It is still refused.** It loses 9.9% on the tune half and
gains 23.7% on the held-out half — it fails on one half and wins on the other
by a similar margin, which is a coin landing differently twice, not a signal.

The bar on this page was written more loosely than the one in
`FINAL_CALL_CANDIDATES.md` two weeks earlier, which correctly required a rule
to beat the benchmark **on both halves** because "one half is a coin toss
with two sides". That omission this morning would have let noise through.
Named here rather than quietly benefited from.

## THE STOPPING RULE IS INVOKED

No seventh rule. No different word list. No other lens.

**The conclusion: the data this project owns contains no detectable edge.**
Five ideas were tested properly on 2026-09-20 — the mined feature cells,
11,200 market-shape combinations, the beaten-lengths manner proxy, the
"favourite beaten far last time" lead, and these six comment rules. Every one
came back negative, or reversed on data it had not seen.

That is not a failure of effort. It is an answer, and it took a day to get
once the testing was honest. What cannot be done with starting prices and
finishing positions may still be possible with money movement, sectional
times or stable intent — none of which this project owns, all of which cost
real money, and none of which is promised to pay for itself.

**The recommendation is to stop.**
