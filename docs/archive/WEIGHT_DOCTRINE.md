## THE SCALE (refines law 3e's exchange rate)
The BHA's own working rule: **pounds per length = 15 ÷ distance in
furlongs**. So the master's 1lb = 1L guide is EXACT at 1m6f-2m, and the
rate climbs steeply shorter:
| trip | lb per length |
|---|---|
| 5f | ~3.0 |
| 6f | ~2.5 |
| 7f | ~2.1 |
| 1m | ~1.9 |
| 1m2f | ~1.5 |
| 1m4f | ~1.25 |
| 1m6f+ | ~1.0 (the master's guide) |
| hurdles | ~1.0 · chases ~0.75 |
Soft/heavy shrinks the rate ~10-15%; AW counts as fast turf.

## THE HANDICAPPER'S MACHINERY
- 1 rating point = 1lb. Ratings revised weekly, published Tuesday.
- THE WELL-IN WINDOW: win after the weekly cutoff → run again off the OLD
  mark + usually a 6lb Flat penalty. When the win was worth 10lb+, the
  penalised horse is well-in — the receipted reason improving last-time-out
  winners under a penalty keep winning. (External confirmation of the
  well-in lens the master taught 2026-07-26.)
- Winners rise ~6-7lb; losers drop 1-3lb a run. "Out of the handicap" =
  automatically badly treated.

## TOP WEIGHT — the tight lead, quantified
Top weights WIN MORE than naive theory says (they are the class horse) and
the decisive modifier is FIELD SIZE, receipted on database studies:
- fields <=10: top weight strikes ~17-22% · fields 11+: ~9-11%.
- Blind backing still loses ~7p/£ — the market knows. The edge is in the
  READ, not the label. Never veto on weight alone (the master's tight-lead
  teaching, independently confirmed).

## THE REST, IN ONE BREATH
- Mark vs LAST WINNING MARK: at/below it = winnable; +7lb or more demands
  proven new improvement.
- WFA: 3yos vs elders in all-aged handicaps Jul-Oct at 1m+ were officially
  over-treated (BHA's own 2024 review; scale cut 2025). Still a mild plus.
- Apprentice claims: neutral by default (the claim prices in the errors);
  value only in proven trainer-apprentice combos and near-graduation 3lb/5lb.
- GROSS weight within 8st-10st barely matters (Mordin; two academic
  studies: significant but small) — RELATIVE pounds vs rating is the game.
- 5-6f big-field handicaps: DRAW dwarfs weight (studied courses: bottom two
  stalls 28/99 wins vs stalls 11+ at 5/98). Weight reasoning downgraded there.

## MYTHS (shot on sight)
"The handicapper makes them finish together" · "1lb = 1 length everywhere"
(3x wrong in sprints) · "a stone more, can't win" (gross burden myth) ·
"7lb claimer = 7lb free" · "top weight is a negative in itself" (the
negative is big fields) · "a 6lb penalty stops a winner" (often the badge
of a well-in horse).

## TONIGHT'S EXAMS, RE-MARKED BY THE SCIENCE
