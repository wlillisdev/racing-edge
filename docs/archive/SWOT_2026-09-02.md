# SWOT — the horse-picking system, 2026-09-02 (the master's order: "when the bots are out do a SWOT analysis to find areas we have missed and can improve. Remember best horse wins")

Written while the third-wave bots (yardstick coverage, rule coverage matrix,
memory wiring, race selection, blind spots, evidence pipe) were out; their
findings are folded in below each heading with the bot named. Evidence lives in
docs/EDGE_LEDGER.md (2026-09-02, three waves). The anchor over everything: the
master's "best horse wins the race" — the read's job is to find the best horse,
the machinery's job is to make sure the read is done on every horse, in the
right race, with every law, and is marked afterwards.

## STRENGTHS (wired end to end — data → engine → reader → law → test → record)
- The record: pre-off bank, SP settle, write-point guards, voids with reasons, the
  backlog sweep, a text twin, the read's own claims graded (danger, crossed-off,
  own price). Nothing is edited, everything is marked.
- The rulebook in the master's words (35 numbered laws), each pinned; the laws
  that have engine teeth are tested by behaviour (stale anchor, answered raise,
  Zavateri gate, glance gate, shape vote, sitting floor, manner vocabulary).
- The shape book: his race-type memory rebuilt from 1,690 corpus races, now
  voting in race selection AND vetoing a lean in a lottery shape.
- The classroom loop: sittings git-locked result-unseen, his result and
  comments, double autopsy, faults named the same night. Two verdict wins and
  the worst read of the month in one week — the loop is running.
- The corpus and tier-0: 23,339 resulted runners scored against the market
  nightly; the month test before anything is carved.
- Fail-loud plumbing: crash mail from the shell, health with an honest half,
  the sha of the running code in every email.

## WEAKNESSES (known before the bots reported; the bots' additions follow)
- THE BOTS' HEADLINE (P2): the rulebook was never sent to the model in the
  default mode — fixed tonight; and the engine's per-horse yardstick was never
  shown to the reader (P1) — fixed tonight. Both are why "we apply some rules,
  not all": the words and the measurements lived in rooms the read never
  entered.
- THE MARKET IS ONE NUMBER (P6): morning == consensus, no late price, no SP on
  the card — laws 4b/4c/4d/4g have no data at all, not merely stale data.
- THE RANK KEY IS RACE-FIRST TO THE EXCLUSION OF THE HORSE (P4): conviction is
  never reached across races; the cornered day still banks (P4) — both for the
  master's ruling.
- MOST LAWS ARE PROMPT-ONLY. The model is told; the engine does not compute them
  (draw 3f, pound-a-length 3e, the five-part solid test 2b-ii, class is
  permanent 3c, track-knows-its-own 3h, the traveller 4f, the bookie's gift 4g,
  the species question 3i). A law the engine cannot measure is a law the record
  cannot grade.
- CONFIDENT IS NEARLY EXTINCT: zero cautions + mark read. Every burn added a
  caution; the bar never learned to stand a caution down except the answered
  raise. The master gets "declinable" daily and decides himself.
- RACE-FIRST CAN OUTRANK THE BEST HORSE: `_rank_key` puts race_quality before
  conviction — a conviction-1 horse in a Class 3 outranks a clean-sheet
  conviction-4 in a Class 5. "Best horse wins" and "right race first" are in
  tension in the arithmetic; the master has not ruled on the tie.
- THE 07:30 PRICE IS NOT THE SP: the shape cell, the fav line and the profile
  all key on a consensus price hours before the off; the book was built on SP.
- NO MARKET CHARACTER AT 07:30: the 'mkt-move' column is OWED by construction;
  flip-flop, boost and backed-clean (law 4g) are read by nobody until the 12:30
  guard, which watches only the banked pick's own price.
- NO PACE MAP, NO DRAW ENGINE, NO WEIGHT-FOR-AGE: the card carries draw and
  weight; nothing computes them; the reader is told to consider them.
- THE RACE'S OWN MEMORY (#29, past winners) has no data source in the pipeline.
- THE DEEP READ SEES THREE RACES (cost cap); every other race is read by the
  mechanical engine alone.
- READER-MODE FLOORS ARE DEAD under engine mode (mark sacred, class, anchor) —
  their comments still claim protection they do not give (for the master's
  ruling).
- NUANCES ARE PROSE: the night study's lessons reach the morning as prompt text;
  none changes a gate or a lens.

## OPPORTUNITIES (cheap, data already paid for)
- DROPPED CARD FIELDS: draw, lbs, headgear_run (first-time gear = E9 setup
  restoration!), trainer_14_days, trainer_rtf, past_results_flags (C/D/BF),
  spotlight, prev_trainers (a yard switch), rating_band, stalls position,
  going_detailed, per-bookmaker odds with each-way terms (price DISPERSION at
  07:30 is a boost/outlier tell even without movement).
- THE DELTA LINE (#28): class move, mark move, trip move per runner from fields
  we hold — the Post's "picture", mechanically.
- THE TRAVELLER (4f) mechanically: trainer_location + course → miles; the
  corpus has trainer ids to condition it per yard (the bots' finding: tells
  nominate, per-yard conditioning pays).
- MARKET SNAPSHOTS: data/market_snapshots exists and the 12:30 guard already
  fetches prices — a per-race snapshot at 07:30, 10:30, 12:30 would give the
  board's CHARACTER (steady / drifting / flip-flopping) mechanically, law 4g.
- PAST WINNERS OF THE RACE: results by course + race name across years is one
  results query per race (the school corpus already holds 2026; the API holds
  more) — the race DNA line.
- UNUSED ENDPOINTS: trainer/jockey/owner analyses, dam/sire class and distance
  — paid for; the bots name which have zero callers.
- THE CLASSROOM AT SCALE: the tier-0 pass and the opinions ledger mark every
  race every night; the master's comments are the scarce input — one sitting a
  night, chosen by the largest read-vs-result gap, not by whim.

## THREATS
- SAMPLE: thirty-odd settled naps; every conclusion about the nap column is
  noise until the 50-pick bar. The volume lives in the opinions and tier-0.
- CROWD MAGNETS: the public tells (hot yard, course master) lift strike and
  lose money — a system that learns from strike alone will chase them.
- PROMPT DRIFT: thirty-five laws plus rulings plus lessons in one prompt; the
  model arbitrates contradictions silently (the "bottling" ruling v the
  earned-pass laws). More words is not more discipline.
- THE BOX: one scheduler, one mailer, no external heartbeat; three blind duty
  days this week were plumbing, not reading.
- THE APPRENTICE: mid-turn suspensions and an eagerness to answer when the
  honest answer is a pass — the walls exist because the habit failed.
- COST: the deep read is the expensive tier; every widening of its field is
  paid daily.
